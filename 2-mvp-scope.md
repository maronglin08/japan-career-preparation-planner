# 2. MVP Scope

## Product vision
Japan Career Preparation Planner helps international university students in Japan organize the tasks and skills they need for job hunting. It gives students a simple place to track preparation without requiring advanced technical knowledge.

**V1.0 target date:** December 15, 2026

## Feature priorities

| Priority | Feature | V1.0 scope and reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Must Have** | Job-preparation task list | Users can add, view, complete, and delete preparation tasks. This is the basic planning function and is achievable in a small first version. |
| **Must Have** | Japanese study tracker | Users can record study sessions and learning goals. Japanese ability is important for many jobs in Japan, so this directly supports the target users. |
| **Must Have** | Resume preparation checklist | Users can follow a checklist for preparing a Japanese-style resume (履歴書) and record what is complete. A checklist is more realistic for V1.0 than building a full resume editor. |
| **Post-launch** | Company and internship bookmarks | Users can save company and internship links. This can wait until the core preparation tools work reliably. |
| **Post-launch** | Application and interview tracker | Users can track applications and interview stages. This adds complexity and is not essential to test the first version. |

## MVP success criteria
A student can create and complete preparation tasks, record Japanese study, and follow the resume checklist on a mobile or desktop browser.
