# 4. Risk Matrix

## Highest-risk MVP feature
**Resume preparation checklist**

| Risk area | Description |
|---|---|
| Hazard | Users may mistake the checklist for official or complete advice about Japanese recruitment documents and submit an incorrect resume. |
| Likelihood | Medium |
| Impact | High |
| Mitigation | Keep the feature as a general preparation checklist, use clear labels, and ask users to verify current requirements with each employer or their university career office. Do not claim that the app guarantees a correct 履歴書. |
| Backup / pivot | If the checklist is confusing or difficult to maintain, replace it with a short list of editable personal tasks and links to official university or employer guidance. |

## Review before release
Test the checklist with a small number of students and revise any unclear wording before making V1.0 public.
